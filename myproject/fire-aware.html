Understanding Veld Fires

Veld fires are uncontrolled fires that spread rapidly through grass, shrubs, and vegetation. During hot and dry seasons in the Overstrand region, the risk increases significantly..

Current Fire Risk Status

Current conditions indicate a low fire risk. Continue to stay informed and follow local guidelines.

Moderate

Current conditions indicate a moderate fire risk. Stay alert and follow local guidelines.

High

Fire risk is high. Avoid outdoor activities and report any signs of fire immediately.

Extreme

Extreme fire risk. Stay indoors, avoid all outdoor activities, and follow emergency instructions.

Fire Prevention Tips

  • Avoid open flames during high-risk periods.
  • Do not discard cigarettes butts or matches outdoors.
  • Keep your property clear of dry vegetation.
  • Report any signs of fire immediately to local authorities.
